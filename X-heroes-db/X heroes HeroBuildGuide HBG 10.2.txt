Hero Build Guide v10.2 by Serenity




Hero Build Guide

Below you will find a guide for ideal build options for heroes, based on PVP mode in the game (Union War, Dominator and Transcendence, Infinity, Dimensional Battle, All Arenas), as well as PVE modes like X-War, Galaxy | Faction wanted, Expedition, Space Discovery, Valiant Trials and Lost Land.
These aren't a hard fast must. You do what you can with what you have. Triple double is also always an option. (3x 2-sets). Also important to note that Annihilation at Present is only useful on Pierre, Epeius, Mikaela and Orochi.
Gem talents may very depending on the opponent you're facing.
Never follow a guide blindly. Read your situation and adapt.

Special note: Speed matters for all heroes. It determines turn order. Speed matters more for some than others, based on individual hero skills. If you're facing CC, third talent 3 is advised. Phantom stigmata is at present invalidated by many heroes and skills. As a result of this, it should be considered a bottom priority of listed recommended stigmata.

Team Build Advice for beginners.

Every good team consists of a few things.
1-2 tanks (the ones taking the bulk of the damage. Such as thinker, Paladin, Rockefeller)
1-2 DP S (your damage dealers. Toko, Indira, Laura etc)
2-4 Supports and buffers | Debuffers (like Cui pan who removes CC, Silas who disables the highest attack enemy and Cupid who buffs the heroes in spots 1,2)

When team building, it's important to remember special boosts you can gain from different compositions. See attached diagrams. Also, Serena (Miss USA) increases ally SUPERMAN FACTION Armor and Attack.

*Faction bonus in star tech adds onto the faction bonus chart. Generally not worth upgrading.

Most DPS heroes you encounter earlier game target the front row, so it's best to put your tanks in the front. Some tanks need to be in position 1. Read each hero's skills. For heroes such as Cui pan, who rely on getting to half life for their special ability, try put them in positions 3 or 4. The DPS, bar those who have counter abilities (like GS) go in the back.




Special note: PVE modes such as void, hub, wish boss, QI have reflectant damage. Meaning the faster you do big damage, the faster you die. Because the damage they deal you directly scales from the damage you do them. Keep that in mind when building your teams.

Acronym List & Definitions
DPS: Damage per second. Your large damage dealers.

CC: Crowd control. Heroes with abilities that prevent enemies from doing things.

Tank: The main damage receiver. There to shield your DPS.

Support: Heals or otherwise affects team in a way that isn't a
constant buff.

Debuffer: Removes buffs or lowers stats of enemy.

Buffer: Increases ally stats in some way.

PVP: Game modes that involve fighting other players.

PVE: Game modes that don't involve fighting other players.

Basic: Basic attack

Ulti: Ultimate attack. Used when energy bar is full. (At 100)


F.A.Q

Why are some heroes recommended for 31 exclusive and not 30? The reason some heroes are recommended for an exclusive of 31 and not 30 is because when you get them to 31, the third stat in their exclusive increases. For example, Alcazar at +30 has additional Armor PEN of 15%. At +31 her additional Armor PEN (the third exclusive stat) increases to 20%.

Why do some heroes have no recommended dark gems? What do I use?
Some heroes don't have recommended dark gems because there is no stat the dark gems provide that they greatly benefit from. Usually in this case, go for speed.

Why are some recommendations highlighted in grey?
The highlights on artifacts and stigmata I personally use on main heroes. (NOTE: This will be how they sit in bag, not how

they should be optimised for union war. If I did it that way, most DPS would have scythe highlighted.)
